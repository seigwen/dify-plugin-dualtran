# Privacy Policy — DualTran for Dify

DualTran translates the text you pass to it. It collects **nothing**: no
analytics, no telemetry, no accounts, and no developer server.

- Text is sent only to the translation service **you** selected: Google
  (default, free, no account) or, with the AI / DeepL engines, directly to the
  provider using the API key stored in the plugin's authorization settings.
- Credentials live in your Dify instance, encrypted like any other plugin
  configuration.
- No other network requests are made by this plugin.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies (Google Translate,
your AI provider, DeepL).
